```text
# 防跨站攻击
请求: http://192.168.50.20:17331/api/v2/websites/crosssite
请求方式: post
开 post数据: {"websiteID":8,"operation":"Enable"}
关 post数据: {"websiteID":8,"operation":"Disable"}
返回: {"code":200,"message":"success","data":null}
```
